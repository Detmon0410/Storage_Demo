  the lot's `quantityOnHand` is now `45` (50 - 5, i.e. the original 20 was restored to 50 before the

  present).
- `DELETE /api/sales-orders/:id` on a fresh order for `quantity: 15` against a lot at 50: after
  delete, confirm the lot's `quantityOnHand` is back to `50`.
